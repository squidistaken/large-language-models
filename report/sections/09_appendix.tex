\section{Language Resource Bands}
\label{app:bands}

Table~\ref{tab:bands} lists the five resource bands from the project brief, which group XED's projected languages by their approximate number of annotated lines; the example languages are illustrative rather than exhaustive. Our assigned language, Croatian, is in band~3.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{XED resource bands and example languages.}
    \label{tab:bands}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lllX}
        \toprule
        Band & Resource level & Annotated lines & Example languages \\
        \midrule
        1 & extreme low & under $\sim$1,000 & Macedonian (300), Vietnamese (956), Slovak (975), Icelandic (977) \\
        2 & very low & $\sim$1,000--4,400 & Danish, Russian, Bosnian, Slovenian, Arabic, Norwegian \\
        3 & mid-low & $\sim$4,400--6,000 & Hebrew, Swedish, Dutch, German, Hungarian, Croatian \\
        4 & mid-high & $\sim$6,000--7,300 & Czech, Italian, Bulgarian, Polish, Portuguese, French \\
        5 & high & $\sim$8,000 and above & Greek, Finnish, Serbian, Turkish, Romanian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
